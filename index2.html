The place itself

What's here

Ten acres, and six places worth walking to. Full descriptions on Visit.

Rare Fruit TrailA frost-free pocket growing things that shouldn't survive in Manatee County — star fruit, dragon fruit, jackfruit, lychee.
Butterfly GardenNectar and host plants chosen to pull butterflies through the season. Best on a warm, still morning.
Serenity GardenBenches, soft planting and a pond just beyond. The place to slow down mid-walk.
Rare Palms & PlumeriaSpecimen palms you won't find in a typical yard, and plumeria that perfume the path in summer.
Art in the ParkThe indoor gallery at the Galleria — rotating shows from local artists, and a cool place to pause.
Hidden Garden NurseryVolunteer-run, near the west entrance. Plants propagated from the collection. Oct–May, Tue & Sat, 9am–noon.

Help document the park

See something interesting? Photograph it and add the observation to the park's iNaturalist project. Naturalists around the world help put a name to it, and your photograph — with your credit on it — may end up on a sign, on this website, or on the screen in the Galleria.

A park built by its community

Palma Sola Botanical Park exists because local people decided a former county nursery was worth saving. It has run as a non-profit since 1993 with no federal, state or local government subsidy — volunteers do nearly everything else.
