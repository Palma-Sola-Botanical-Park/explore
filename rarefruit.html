What is here

Every rare-fruit species photographed in the quadrant, with how many observations stand behind it. Gold means it has a pin in placements.json; the rest are known from photographs but have never had a coordinate recorded.

    Built from the palma-sola-botanical-park iNaturalist project and the park's own catalogue. Positions are the median of each species' best-placed observations, so a dot marks roughly where that fruit is, not a specific tree — several species have more than one plant here.

    The MRFC list is a hunt list, not an inventory. Randy: “I know I've found things in their area not listed, and vice versa.” Absence from this map means nobody has photographed it, which is not the same as absence from the park.
