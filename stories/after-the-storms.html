After the storms

Three hurricanes in two seasons, and what each one left behind.
October 2024

SAMPLE TEXT. Wind damage is loud and quick: a palm across the road, a trellis down, a week of chainsaws. Salt damage is quiet and slow, and it was months before anyone connected the dying pond edges to the one storm that had pushed the bay over the bank.

SAMPLE TEXT. The cleanup crew was the usual one, plus everyone who turned up. The burn pile grew for a month.

The trees we lost to wind, we could see. The ones we lost to salt we did not know about until spring.
