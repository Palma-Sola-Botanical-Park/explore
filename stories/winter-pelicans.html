The American White Pelicans come in December and leave before the heat. The Big Pond is on their route.
January 2026

SAMPLE TEXT. They are enormous, and they are quiet. A flock of American White Pelicans drops onto the Big Pond some winters and spends a few days there, feeding in formation: a line of birds swimming slowly toward the bank, driving the fish ahead of them, then dipping together.

SAMPLE TEXT. They breed on lakes in the northern plains and Canada and spend the winter on the Gulf coast. The park is a stopover, not a home: a safe pond with fish in it, a few hundred yards from the bay.
