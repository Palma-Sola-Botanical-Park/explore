Protecting our pond banks

Three ponds, one storm, and the slow work of putting the edges back.

SAMPLE TEXT. When Hurricane Helene pushed salt water into the park in September 2024, the pond edges took the worst of it. What grew there died back, the banks began to slump, and the ponds lost the green rim that had held them for years.

This is the story of what came after: the grants that paid for rock and plants, the people who waded in, and what the banks look like now. It is told in pieces, because it happened in pieces. Read them in any order.
