The palm collection nobody knew we had

Decades of quiet buying at estate sales left us with one of the better palm collections on the coast.
September 2026

SAMPLE TEXT. Nobody set out to build a palm collection. For years one volunteer went to the plant sales at the Edison and Ford estates in Fort Myers and came back with whatever was rare, small and cheap. The small things grew.

SAMPLE TEXT. The Royal Palm field is the obvious part. The less obvious part is scattered through the rest of the park: a silver palm from Hispaniola here, a cluster palm from the Caribbean there, each with a number painted on a stake that only one person could read.

SAMPLE TEXT. In 2026 we started walking the palms with the person who planted them, matching numbers to names, and giving each one a page of its own. This story is the one place that history is told; the palm pages point here instead of repeating it.
