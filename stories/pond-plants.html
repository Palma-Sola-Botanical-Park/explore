The plants we chose, and why

Salt wind above, wet feet below, and nothing that needs a hose.
March 2026 · Part of Protecting our pond banks

SAMPLE TEXT. A pond bank is two gardens in one. The bottom strip floods after every heavy rain and dries out between. The top strip never floods but takes the salt wind straight off the bay. No single plant is happy in both, so the planting is in rows.

SAMPLE TEXT. Scorpion Tail went in along the top. It shrugs off salty wind but will not stand in brackish water, so it stays above the rock. Its little curled flower spikes bring in the small butterflies all year, which was not the point but is the part visitors notice.

Species pageScorpion Tail

SAMPLE TEXT. Nothing in the planting needs a hose once it is established. That was the second rule after salt: a bank that needs watering every week is a bank that will be bare again the first summer someone is away.
