Why the banks needed work

Salt water does not look like damage until the following spring.
March 2025 · Part of Protecting our pond banks

SAMPLE TEXT. The surge came in overnight and was gone by morning. For a few weeks the park looked almost normal. Then the plants along the water began to brown from the roots up, and by spring the banks that had been held by a tangle of stems were bare soil sliding toward the water.

SAMPLE TEXT. A bare bank is a problem that gets worse on its own. Every rain carries a little more soil into the pond, the water clouds, and the edge creeps back toward the path. The fix is the same one the estuary programs recommend everywhere on the bay: rock where the waves hit, and deep-rooted plants above the rock to hold what is left.

You could stand on the bank and feel it move under you. That was the day we knew it was not going to fix itself.

Randy Carter spring 2025

SAMPLE TEXT. Two grants, from the Tampa Bay and Sarasota Bay estuary programs, paid for the limestone and the first round of native plants. The labor was ours.
