Looking at a screen
  • Every screen is meant to fill a TV. In a browser, press F11 (Windows) or Control-Command-F (Mac) for full screen; press it again to come back.
  • They change slides slowly on purpose. To see a whole rotation quickly, add ?speed=5 to the end of the address.
  • Gold text in [square brackets] on an event screen is a detail still to be filled in — where the vendors are, where Santa sits. Tell Randy and it goes in.

The everyday screens read the Google Sheet every ten minutes, so an edit there reaches the TV within about an hour. The event screens are fixed pages and change only when someone edits them.
