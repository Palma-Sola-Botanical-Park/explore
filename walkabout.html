The walkabout

Everything the group photographed, newest first. Click any picture to open it on iNaturalist.

Who was out

What each person recorded. The map pin figure is how precisely their observations were placed — under 10 metres means the pin was put on the actual plant.

A first for the park

Species with no record anywhere in the park’s catalogue — not on a plant page, not in the research file, not among the wildlife. If one of these holds up, somebody found something the park has never documented.

Seen more than once

The same species recorded more than once on this walkabout. Some of those are genuinely different plants in different places — and some are the group crowding round the same one. Click a name to see the photographs side by side and tell which.

Still to identify

Recorded on the walkabout but not yet named to a species. This is the pile to work through together on the screen.

Known here, research not completed yet

The park knows it has these, but they have no finished page on the website yet. These photographs may be what gets one written.

Where we walked

Every observation with a location, on the satellite view. Empty ground is somewhere nobody walked.

Starting…

Live from iNaturalist, covering the last 4 hours, by the time each observation was made, for the palma-sola-botanical-park project. Anything still on somebody’s phone will appear here once they upload it — and an observation with no time recorded is kept rather than dropped, so a few may sit outside the window.

The Walkabout list is built from the project itself: any day in the last five months with twelve or more observations. Choosing one jumps to that day and lands the window on its last observation.
