Orchid Tree

Bauhinia variegata
On this page

At a glance

  • The leaf is a single blade with a notch at the tip that makes two rounded lobes. The tree's scientific name, Bauhinia, honors two Swiss botanist brothers, and in 1737 the Swedish botanist Linnaeus wrote that the two-lobed leaf stands for the pair.
  • The flower on Hong Kong's flag comes from a hybrid orchid tree that sets no seed. Every Hong Kong orchid tree is thought to be a cutting or graft of a single tree found there in the 1880s.
  • In northern India and Nepal the flower buds of the orchid tree are cooked as a vegetable and pickled.

Cultural significance

Two brothers

Johann Bauhin was born in 1541 and Caspar Bauhin in 1560, so they were not twins, as is sometimes said. Plumier gave the genus their name in 1703, calling them a noble pair of brothers.

A flower on a flag

The Hong Kong orchid tree was named in 1908 for a governor of the colony, became the city's flower in 1965 and went onto the regional flag in 1997. On the flag the flower is a white outline on red; the living flower is pink-purple.

Where it comes from

India to China

Linnaeus described the species in 1753 from India's Malabar coast. Its native range runs from India across Southeast Asia to southern China. His description calls the petals rose, blotched with yellow and purple, and the species name, variegata, means variegated.

A street tree for narrow streets

A Manatee County nursery was selling orchid trees by 1888. By the 1920s its catalog recommended them for narrow streets, for their large two-lobed leaves and weeks of bloom.

What it does here

February flowers

In the first week of February 2025 the tree in the photos was in full flower, with green leaves still on its branches.

Bees come to it

In a study in Hong Kong, honey bees were the most frequent visitors to orchid tree flowers. Carpenter bees, a swallowtail butterfly and even a squirrel came too.

A bean-family cousin

Royal Poinciana is another bean-family tree. Poinciana divides its leaves into many small leaflets, while an orchid tree leaf is one broad blade with a notch. The two show how different bean-family trees can look.

How to know it

An orchid-like flower

Five petals spread around long, curved stamens, with one petal marked in a deeper color. The flowers are pink-purple to magenta and several inches across.

A broad, notched leaf

The leaf is heart-shaped at its base and notched at the tip, gray-green, with veins fanning out from the stalk. It is sometimes called the camel's foot.

When to look

In Florida the orchid tree flowers from about January into spring.
