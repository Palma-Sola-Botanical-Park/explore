Dwarf Poinciana

At a glance

  • The flowers look like small explosions of red, orange and yellow, with long stamens shooting out past the petals.
  • This is the Pride of Barbados, the national flower of Barbados and part of the country's coat of arms.
  • Despite the name it stays a shrub or small tree, not the huge canopy tree the Royal Poinciana becomes.
  • The ripe seeds are poisonous. Leave the pods alone.

Cultural significance

Barbados put it on the shield

Two of its flowers appear on the Barbados coat of arms, and the plant is so tied to the island that one of its common names is simply Pride of Barbados.

Where it comes from

Mexico and Central America

Native from southeastern Mexico through Central America, and grown across the tropical world for so long that its exact origin was once a puzzle. Linnaeus named it pulcherrima, "most beautiful", in 1753, and the name has stuck.

Where to find it here

Across the drive from the office

On the Front Pond, directly across the driveway from the office. A rounded shrub with fine ferny leaves and, most of the year, a few red-and-orange flower spikes standing up out of the top.

What it does here

The poinciana look at shrub height

It gives the fiery poinciana flowers at a height where you can see them up close, instead of forty feet overhead. Butterflies and hummingbirds visit.

How to know it

Long stamens steal the show

They reach well past the petals and give each flower a loose, feathery look.

Ferny leaves

Twice-divided, with many tiny leaflets, so the whole shrub looks soft.

Flat pods

Flowers are followed by flat bean pods that darken as they ripen.

How it grows

Heat and sun

It flowers best in bright warm weather. A frost may knock it back, and it resprouts from the roots.

Takes pruning

Easy to keep as a shrub even though it can grow into a small tree.

Pods mean seedlings

Leave the pods on and it can reseed.

Take care

Leave the seeds alone

The ripe seeds are poisonous if eaten. It is an ornamental, not a food plant, and dogs that eat the pods can get sick too.
